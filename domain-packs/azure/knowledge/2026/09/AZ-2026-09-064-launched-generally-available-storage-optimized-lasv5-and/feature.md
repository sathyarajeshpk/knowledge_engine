# [Launched] Generally Available: Storage optimized Lasv5 and Laosv5 Azure VM series

The Laosv5 series and Lasv5 storage optimized VMs, based on the 5th Gen AMD EPYC™ processor (Turin), are now generally available.Lasv5 and Laosv5 VMs have sizes ranging from 2-160 vCPUs, with 8GiB of memory and 720GB of local NVMe disk capacity per vCPU.

Source: [azure-updates](https://azure.microsoft.com/updates?id=572630)
