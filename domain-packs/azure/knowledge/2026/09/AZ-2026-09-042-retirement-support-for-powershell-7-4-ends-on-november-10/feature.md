# Retirement: Support for PowerShell 7.4 ends on November 10, 2026

Support for PowerShell 7.4 will end on November 10, 2026, please transition to PowerShell 7.6. Your Azure Function apps will continue to run, but security patches and updates will no longer be provided and customer support for PowerShell 7.4 will be disco

Source: [azure-updates](https://azure.microsoft.com/updates?id=572770)
