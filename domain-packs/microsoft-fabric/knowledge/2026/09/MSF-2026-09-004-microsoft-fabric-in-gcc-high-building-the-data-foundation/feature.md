# Microsoft Fabric in GCC High: Building the data foundation for AI

Government organizations are ready to move beyond using AI to answer questions and toward intelligent systems and agents that can take action. However, data is often distributed across clouds, databases, applications, and operational systems, limiting access to the business context and organizational knowledge these systems need to make informed decisions. Today, we are excited to announce the availability of Microsoft Fabric in Microsoft 365 Government Community Cloud High (GCC High). Beginning September 2, 2026, Microsoft Fabric will be available in public preview for GCC High customers, with general availability starting October 1, 2026.
