# ADBC drivers for supported Power BI and Fabric connectors

ODBC to ADBC driver transition (Generally Available) ADBC drivers for supported Power BI and Fabric connectors are available for validation before they become the default and embedded ODBC drivers are disabled. For more information, see Transition from ODBC to ADBC drivers.

Source: [fabric-whats-new](https://community.fabric.microsoft.com/t5/Fabric-Updates-Blog/Start-validating-today-The-ODBC-to-ADBC-driver-transition-in/ba-p/5362917)
