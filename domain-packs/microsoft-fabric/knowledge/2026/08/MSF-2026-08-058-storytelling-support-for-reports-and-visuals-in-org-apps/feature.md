# Storytelling support for reports and visuals in org apps

Reports in org apps now support Storytelling in PowerPoint, allowing users to add live report pages and visuals from org app content into PowerPoint presentations. For more information, see Add live Power BI data to PowerPoint.

Source: [powerbi-whats-new-markdown](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/power-bi/collaborate-share/service-power-bi-powerpoint-add-in-install)
