# View org apps in the Power BI mobile apps

Org app audiences are now in mobile apps Org app audiences in the Power BI mobile app extend a tailored org app experience to users on iOS and Android. Mobile and tablet users can open an org app and see content based on their assigned audience, delivering the right content to the right people on smaller screens. For more information, see View org apps in the Power BI mobile apps.
