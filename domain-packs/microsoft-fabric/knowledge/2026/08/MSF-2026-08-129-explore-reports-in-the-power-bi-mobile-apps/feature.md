# Explore reports in the Power BI mobile apps

Rotate view in the mobile report footer The Rotate view button in the Power BI Mobile report footer lets you switch layouts with one tap, even when the phone is locked in portrait orientation. Rolling out to iOS and Android. For more information, see Explore reports in the Power BI mobile apps.
