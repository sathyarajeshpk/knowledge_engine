# Workspace-level surge protection (Preview)

Workspace-level surge protection controls (Preview) enable per-workspace CU percentage limits over a rolling 24-hour period, automatic blocking of workspaces exceeding thresholds, and mission critical mode to exempt high-priority workspaces from surge protection rules. For more information, see Surge protection.
