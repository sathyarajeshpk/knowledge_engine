The Rayfin AMA recap summarizes the community's top questions from a recent Reddit AMA, covering how to combine analytics, writeback, and operational workflows on Fabric data; upcoming Functions and connector investments for external APIs, email, and Work IQ; row-level security enforcement through semantic models; and planned first-class TypeScript Functions, secret management, version control, and Entra ID group support.
