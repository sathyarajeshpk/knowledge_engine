# Job concurrency and queue monitoring (Generally Available)

Spark job monitoring (Generally Available) Job concurrency and queue monitoring (Generally Available) adds a capacity-level view so you can compare Spark resource use across workspaces, identify contention, and tune limits. For more information, see Job concurrency and queue monitoring and Concurrency limits and queueing in Apache Spark for Fabric.
