# OneLake architectural guidance for the AI era

OneLake architectural guidance is a blueprint that connects business priorities to architecture decisions through strategic initiatives, architectural patterns, and platform capabilities, with five repeatable patterns: data access unification, medallion architecture, data mesh with governance, platform simplification, and external data sharing.
