# OneLake storage tiers and lifecycle management

OneLake storage tiers and lifecycle management (Generally Available) OneLake storage tiers and lifecycle management let you keep active data in the hot tier and move less-used data to cool or cold tiers with lifecycle policies. For more information, see Get the size of OneLake items and OneLake diagnostics.
