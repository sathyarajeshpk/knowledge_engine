# Customer-intent streaming pattern

Customer-intent streaming pattern shows how you capture CDC events in Eventstream, reshape them with DeltaFlow (Preview), enrich micro-batches with AI Functions, publish Business Events, and activate responses while the signal remains current. For more information, see AI Functions for PySpark reference and Business events overview.
