# Manage surge protection for Fabric capacities

Workspace-level surge protection (Preview) Workspace-level surge protection lets capacity admins cap a workspace's compute consumption, automatically or manually block excessive consumers, exempt mission-critical workspaces, and restore blocked workspaces. For more information, see Manage surge protection for Fabric capacities.

Source: [fabric-whats-new](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/fabric/enterprise/surge-protection)
