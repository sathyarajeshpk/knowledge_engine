# Fabric Lakehouse support in dbt job (Preview)

Fabric Lakehouse support in dbt job (Preview) lets you run dbt projects directly against a new or existing lakehouse and apply dbt models and tests where your data is stored. For more information, see dbt job in Microsoft Fabric.

Source: [fabric-whats-new](https://community.fabric.microsoft.com/blog/fbc_fabricupdatesblogs/introducing-fabric-lakehouse-support-in-dbt-job-for-microsoft-fabric-preview/5358240)
