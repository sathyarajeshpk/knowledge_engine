# Real-Time Dashboard-specific visuals

Time Series Visualization in Real-Time Dashboards (Preview) Real-Time Dashboard-specific visuals in Real-Time Dashboards adds dedicated capabilities for navigating, comparing, and customizing time-based data, including a legend search bar, pin-and-overlay for series comparison, multiple aligned-time panels, flexible Y-axis scaling, color assignment, and zoom with a time slider.

Source: [fabric-whats-new-markdown](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/fabric/real-time-intelligence/dashboard-visuals)
