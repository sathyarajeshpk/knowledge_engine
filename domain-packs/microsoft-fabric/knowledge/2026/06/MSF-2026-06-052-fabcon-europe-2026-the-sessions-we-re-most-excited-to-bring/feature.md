# FabCon Europe 2026: The sessions we’re most excited to bring to Barcelona

There’s nothing quite like a room full of people who love data as much as you do. We can’t wait to reconnect with this community in Barcelona. The European Microsoft Fabric + SQL Community Conference (FabCon and SQLCon Europe) returns September 28-October 1, 2026, at the Centre de Convencions Internacional de Barcelona. After a year of incredible innovation across Microsoft Fabric and SQL, this conference is shaping up to be something special. We have a lot to share—and even more to learn from each other. Following our record-breaking gathering of more than 4,000 data professionals in Vienna last year, FabCon Europe is back and bigger—with 130+ expert-led sessions…
