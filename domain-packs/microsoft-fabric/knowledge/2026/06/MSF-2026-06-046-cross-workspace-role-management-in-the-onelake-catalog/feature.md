# Cross-workspace role management in the OneLake catalog (Preview)

Cross-workspace role management in the OneLake catalog (Preview) lets you assign, edit, and remove members for compatible workspace roles and OneLake security roles across multiple workspaces from the Secure tab in one workflow, instead of updating each workspace individually. For more information, see OneLake catalog overview and Secure your Fabric data.
