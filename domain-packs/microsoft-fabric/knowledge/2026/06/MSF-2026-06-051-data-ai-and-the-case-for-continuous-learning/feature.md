# Data, AI, and the case for continuous learning

Learning has never been more important for data and AI professionals. As AI rapidly shapes how organizations build, analyze and act on data, staying current is no longer optional; it’s essential for both career growth and business success. Data professionals have always worked in a fast-moving field, but the pace today is fundamentally different. Every day, the world generates roughly 400 million terabytes of data, and nearly 90% of the world’s data has been created in just the last two years.
