# Capacity metrics app enhancements and Chargeback app (Generally Available)

Capacity health page, timepoint summary, timepoint detail, and the Chargeback app are generally available. The health page surfaces utilization and throttling signals across all capacities per region, timepoint summary, and detail break down compute usage by workspace, item, operation, and user, and the Chargeback app supports cost allocation by SKU and workload type. For more information, see Understand the metrics app Health page and Microsoft Fabric Chargeback app.
