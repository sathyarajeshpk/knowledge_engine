# OneLake storage tiers and lifecycle management (Preview)

OneLake now supports hot, cool, and cold storage tiers, and lifecycle management policies automatically move files between tiers based on creation time, last modification, or last access, so you trade higher transaction and retrieval costs for lower storage cost on infrequently accessed data.
