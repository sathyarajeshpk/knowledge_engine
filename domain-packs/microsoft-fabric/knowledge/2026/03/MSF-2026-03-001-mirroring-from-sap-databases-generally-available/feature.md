# Mirroring SAP

Mirroring from SAP databases (Generally Available) You can continuously replicate your SAP data directly into Fabric's OneLake. Once in Fabric, you can take advantage of powerful capabilities for business intelligence, AI, data engineering, data science, and data sharing. For more information, see Mirroring SAP.

Source: [fabric-whats-new-markdown](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/fabric/mirroring/sap)
